Adds the *Generate complementary quotation* button to the repair order:
the items awaiting approval (see ``repair_item_approval``) go to a new
sale order linked to the same repair order, which inherits the customer,
pricelist, payment term, fiscal position, salesperson, team and sale
order type of the main order.

Confirming the complementary order (backend or customer portal) approves
its items: they reserve stock and are consumed by the repair order.
Cancelling or refusing it refuses the items, which leave the repair
order and unblock its conclusion. The method
``_prepare_complementary_order_vals`` is the extension point for other
modules to make the complementary order inherit more fields.

Configuration
=============

Add the users who generate and send complementary orders to the group
*Repair complementary quotation* (it implies the salesman group).

Usage
=====

1. Items added in the repair order above the approval limit stay
   pending.
2. In the repair order, press *Generate complementary quotation*: a new
   sale order opens with one line per pending item.
3. Check prices and send it. The customer accepts it in the portal or
   the salesperson confirms it: the items join the repair order. If it
   is cancelled or refused, the items are refused.
