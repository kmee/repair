Adds a "repair mode" to the sale order type. For the types set to "One
repair order per sale order", confirming a sale order opens a single
repair order (instead of one per line) carrying:

- the goods of the order as "Add" parts, linked to their sale line and
  without any delivery (the repair order reserves and consumes them);
- the services of the order in the "Services" tab, linked to their sale
  line.

Lines added or changed later in the confirmed sale order are
synchronised to the repair order, and parts and services added in the
repair order reach the sale order. Cancelling, copying, delivered
quantities and the access of salespeople to the repair order are
handled.

Configuration
=============

In *Sales > Configuration > Sale Order Types*, set **Repair Mode** to
"One repair order per sale order" on the types that must open a repair
order (for example "Maintenance"). Optionally choose the repair
operation type; empty uses the repair operation type of the warehouse of
the sale order.

Usage
=====

1. Create a sale order of a type with repair mode enabled, with storable
   goods and services.
2. Confirm it: a single repair order is created, no delivery is
   generated. The goods are reserved by the repair order and the
   services appear in its "Services" tab.
3. Complete the repair order: the parts are consumed and the delivered
   quantity of the sale lines is updated, services included.
