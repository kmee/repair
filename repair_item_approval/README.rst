Adds an approval step to the items (parts and services) that the
technician adds in a repair order served by a sale order in repair mode
(see ``sale_repair_order``).

- An item below the limit is applied directly. The limit is an amount in
  a configurable currency, set on the company (default USD 100),
  compared with the line total ("below", strict, by default) and
  converted with the rate of the day.
- A customer flagged "approves every repair item" gets every added item
  waiting for approval.
- A pending item does not reserve stock, is not consumed and does not
  reach the sale order. The salesperson of the order receives an
  activity.
- The repair order cannot be finished with pending items.
- Items have a class: standard, mandatory by the regulation (price and
  discount locked, refusal needs a note) or recommended.

Configuration
=============

In the company form, tab *Repair Approval*: currency and amount of the
limit, whether it applies to the line total or to the unit price,
whether an item equal to the limit is applied directly, and whether the
items of the same product are summed. On the customer, tick *Approves
Every Repair Item* when needed. Grant *Repair item approval manager* to
who may approve items without the customer.

Usage
=====

1. In a repair order created by a sale order of repair mode, add a part
   or a service. Below the limit it is applied and reaches the sale
   order; above it shows as *Awaiting Approval* and the salesperson gets
   an activity.
2. A manager approves it internally (with a note) or the pending items
   are refused (a note is required for mandatory items).
3. The repair order cannot be finished while items are pending.
